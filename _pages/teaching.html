Teaching & Mentoring

Teaching measurement as a way of reasoning.

Most of my teaching has happened at the instrument: lab courses, facility training and one-on-one mentoring of PhD students. I want students to leave able to choose a measurement for a question, recognize an artifact, and report data that someone else can reproduce.

Courses I am prepared to teach

Polymer science

Synthesis, chain statistics, thermal transitions and viscoelasticity.

Materials characterization

Microscopy, spectroscopy and scanning probe methods, with an emphasis on artifacts and reporting.

Mechanics of soft materials

Elasticity, viscoelasticity and contact mechanics of polymers and gels.

Surfaces and interfaces

Surface chemistry, polymer brushes, adhesion and thin films.

Measurement and instrumentation lab

A hands-on course in which students design, calibrate and validate a measurement from start to finish.
